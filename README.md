# KullThranUI Forever

### A familiar home for your interface in WoW Forever.

[Website](https://kullthranui.com/) · [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/kullthranui-forever) · [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/kullthranui-forever) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/cqAVWpeVvd)

KullThranUI Forever brings KullThranUI's approach to interface customisation to **WoW Forever**, with an edition adapted to that client.

Its modules let you decide how much of your interface you want to change. Pick a visual style, adjust your layout and colours, and build something that feels comfortable to play with. You can start small and make it your own over time.

Visit the [website](https://kullthranui.com/) for screenshots and a closer look at the project.

## Download and install

Get the Forever edition from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/kullthranui-forever), [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/kullthranui-forever), or the packaged addon attached to a [GitHub release](https://github.com/KullThran/KullThranUI-Forever/releases).

For a manual installation, close the game and extract all `KullThranUI` and `KullThranUI_*` folders from the addon package directly into your Forever client's `Interface/AddOns` folder. Keep them side by side, without an extra enclosing folder, then enable them in the character selection screen's AddOns menu.

## Getting started

Run `/installer` to open the setup wizard. Use `/kt` or `/kui` whenever you want to open the options and make further adjustments.
